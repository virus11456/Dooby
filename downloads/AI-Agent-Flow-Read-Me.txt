AI Agent Flow 0.3.9 — macOS 13+ / Apple Silicon

安裝：把 App 拖曳到 Applications。
Claude：在官方 Claude Code 登入，再於 AI Flow 連接官方摘要。
AI Flow 不讀取 Claude 鑰匙圈或更新其憑證。完成一次 Claude Code 對話後才有摘要；只用網頁聊天時可能不更新。

Install: Drag the app onto Applications.
Claude: Sign in in official Claude Code, then connect the official summary in AI Flow.
AI Flow does not read Claude Keychain credentials or renew its tokens. A Claude Code interaction is needed to provide a summary; web-only activity may not update it.
